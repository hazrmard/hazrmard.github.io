Why 2q ≥ N Is a Hard Floor

Sorting n = 3 items (A, B, C) has N = 3! = 6 possible answers. Each leaf is one transcript of Y/N answers.

q = 2 questions → 22 = 4 < 6 Fungible answers

Only 4 transcripts exist for 6 orderings—at least two distinct answers share an identical Y/N history and become fungible.

q = 3 questions → 23 = 8 ≥ 6 All 6 separated

With 3 questions (⌈log2 6⌉ = 3), 8 transcripts give every single ordering its own unambiguous leaf.
