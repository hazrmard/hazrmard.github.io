## Can't escape heat

In space, there is no air. The only means of expelling heat is radiation. Thermal radiators can be used to absorb heat from the computer, and then emit it into space. The fundamental conservation of energy must be conserved.

Heat in = heat out

Where, the heat into the radiator is the power generated by the computer, $P$.
